O canal é o seu quintalThe canal is your backyard

A casa ocupa a ponta de um lote de esquina sobre o canal do Rio Camboriú, com acesso náutico direto ao mar. Piscina com hidromassagem, deck de madeira e coqueiros, tudo de frente para a água, e um projeto de píer pronto para receber sua frota.

The home sits on a corner lot on the Camboriú River canal, with direct boating access to the ocean. Pool with spa, wood deck and palm trees, all facing the water, plus dock plans ready for your boats.

The main level is built for gatherings: living room with fireplace, dining for 14, charcoal and gas grills, a 522-bottle wine cellar and sound in every room. Everything opens to the covered terrace, the pool and the canal.
